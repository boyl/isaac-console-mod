# Third-party fonts

## Fusion Pixel Font

- Project: <https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font>
- Author/maintainer: TakWolf and project contributors
- Bundled assets: 10px and 12px BMFont editions
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1
- Reserved font name: `Fusion Pixel`

The complete license is included as `FONT-LICENSE-OFL.txt`. Console UI distributes the font files under that license and does not claim ownership of the font.

## HD fonts: Source Han Sans

- Source: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-sans
- Pinned revision: a4f7cf94edfb9d7ffbdfc4841de276358bd7e0f2
- Regular / Medium; logical sizes 12 / 10 / 10, rasterized at twice the resolution and drawn at 0.5 scale.
- Derived name: Isaac Console HD. License: resources/font/hd/LICENSE-OFL.txt.
- Reproducible build and hashes: tools/fetch-hd-fonts.ps1, tools/build_hd_fonts.py and resources/font/hd/manifest.json.
